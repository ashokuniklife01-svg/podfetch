WhatsApp

How to save a Status.

A website cannot reach the statuses on your phone. Use these steps on the device where you already watched the Status.

Android

From Files, or share it

  1. Open Files (or any file manager) and go to WhatsApp → Media → .Statuses. Recent statuses you have already opened are often stored there for a short time. Copy the photo or video somewhere safe.
  2. Or open the Status in WhatsApp, tap Share or Forward, and send it to yourself (or to Saved messages / a personal chat).
iPhone

Forward, or screen record

  1. Open the Status and use Forward to send it to yourself.
  2. If there is no forward option, use iOS Screen Recording (Control Center) while the Status plays, or take a screenshot for a photo Status.

WhatsApp only keeps a Status for 24 hours. Save it while it is still visible. This app does not download WhatsApp statuses from a link.
